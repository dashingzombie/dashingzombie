## 🌱 About me

- 🔬 I am a **PhD Fellow at Aarhus University** working at the intersection of machine learning, computer vision, behavioural ecology, and biodiversity monitoring.
- 🪱 My current research focuses on **automated earthworm identification and phenotyping** from images.
- 🧠 I develop **multitask and hierarchical deep-learning models** for predicting taxonomic and biological traits.
- 🛠️ I build custom **software, imaging systems, sensors, and experimental platforms** for ecological research.
- 📊 I am interested in interpretable machine learning, experimental design, and reproducible computational workflows.
- 💬 Ask me about **PyTorch, computer vision, ecological machine learning, automated imaging, or research hardware**.
- 📫 Contact me at **[devd@qgg.au.dk](mailto:devd@qgg.au.dk)**.
